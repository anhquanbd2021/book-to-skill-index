Book Index · manifest + leaves

One question. Two ways to pay for the context.

The corpus is The Queue at the Edge — a fixture book of six chapters. Ask it a question two ways: dump every chapter into context, or consult a SKILL.md-style manifest and load only the one chapter file it points to. Then ask the question the distiller dropped.

Safe educational demo. The book is a fixture, "answering" is evidence lookup, and token counts are ~4-chars-per-token estimates — the lab demonstrates cost and routing, not an LLM.
