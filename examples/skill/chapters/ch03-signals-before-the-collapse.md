# ch03 — Signals Before the Collapse (distilled)

The three predictive signals, in order of lead time:
1. Queue-depth SLOPE (derivative), not depth — a positive slope held
   ~90s precedes every collapse in the chapter.
2. Retry amplification — work-items vs offered-requests ratio; >~1.3
   means the queue is feeding on itself.
3. Accept-rate drift — front-door accept rate sagging (99.8% → 97%)
   precedes visible errors.

Rule kept: one signal is weather, two is a forecast, three is a
collapse in progress. Source ref: Part II, §3.1–3.5.
